Questions

The honest answers.

Does Finmate connect to my bank? ?

No. Finmate is fully manual — you enter your transactions and balances yourself. This is intentional. No bank credentials, no data sharing, no third-party access to your accounts. Your financial data never leaves your device except to your private Supabase account.

Is it actually free? What's the catch? ?

The core app — dashboard, transactions, budgets, debt tracker — is free. We show non-intrusive ads. AI Insights will be a premium feature when it launches in V1.1. There's no hidden paywall blocking the tools that matter most.

Is my data private and secure? ?

Yes. All data is stored in a private, authenticated Supabase database with row-level security — meaning only your account can access your data. Financial amounts are never logged in analytics or error tracking. Biometric authentication is supported on all devices.

When is AI Insights coming? ?

AI Insights is the next major feature — coming in V1.1. It includes a 30-day balance forecast, personalized spending alerts, and a chat interface that answers questions about your actual financial patterns. Download now and you'll get it automatically when it drops.

Is it available on Android? ?

Yes! Finmate is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Download free on either platform — your data is private, stays in your account, and everything syncs across devices.

What if I want to export my data? ?

CSV and PDF data export is on the roadmap for V1.1. Your data is always accessible through the app, and because it's stored in a standard Postgres database, export will be straightforward when the feature ships.
