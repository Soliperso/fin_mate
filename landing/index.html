Questions

The honest answers.

Does Finmate connect to my bank? +

No. Finmate is fully manual — you enter your transactions and balances yourself. This is intentional. No bank credentials, no data sharing, no third-party access to your accounts. Your financial data never leaves your device except to your private Supabase account.

Is it actually free? What's the catch? +

The core app — dashboard, transactions, budgets, debt tracker — is free, forever. No hidden paywalls on the tools that matter most. No credit card required to start.

Is my data private and secure? +

Yes. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored in a private authenticated database — only your account can access your data. Financial amounts are never logged in analytics or shared with third parties. Biometric authentication is supported on all devices.

Will there be AI features? +

Yes — AI Insights is coming in a future update. It will let you ask questions about your finances and get answers based on your real data, not generic advice. Think 30-day balance forecasting, personalized spending alerts, and a chat interface that knows your actual numbers.

Is it available on Android? +

Finmate is currently available on iOS only (App Store). Android is on the roadmap — follow us for updates on when it launches.

What if I want to export my data? +

CSV and PDF data export is on the roadmap for V1.1. Your data is always accessible through the app, and because it's stored in a standard Postgres database, export will be straightforward when the feature ships.
